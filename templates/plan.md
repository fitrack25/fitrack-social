# Fitrack Instagram plan (read by the weekly scheduled runs)

## Rules
- Audience: gym owners (B2B), until the owner says to mix in members.
- Language: English. Tanglish (Tamil in Latin letters, e.g. "Notebook venam. Fitrack podhum.") in at most ONE of the three posts a week, and skip it entirely every other week. No Tamil script.
- Look: Apple-style. White / #F5F5F7 / pure black backgrounds, huge centred Inter Display headlines (700, tight tracking), one short grey subline, generous whitespace, realistic iPhone mockups with soft shadows showing Fitrack screens. Fitrack Orange #F97316 only as an accent (eyebrow text, one highlight word, buttons). Navy #1F2937. No gradients except a faint orange glow on black. No emoji in images.
- Templates: base.css, carousel.html, poll.html, reel.html, render.js in this folder. Reuse them; change words and phone screens.
- Only real Fitrack features: members with Active / Expiring / Expired / Frozen status, member profiles, freeze/renew, collecting payments by UPI/cash/card, pending dues, manual attendance check-in by member ID/name/phone, past attendance by date, attendance export to Excel/CSV, reports, several businesses in one app (gyms, swimming pools, dance studios), works on iOS and Android, plan bought on fitrack.in. Never invent statistics, prices, free trials or testimonials. Names and numbers on mock screens are sample data.
- Each week: Monday = carousel (5–7 slides, 1080x1350), Wednesday = reel (12–20 s, 1080x1920 MP4, silent, keep text out of the bottom 380 px), Friday = engagement post (single 1080x1350 image: poll, quiz, this-or-that, tip).
